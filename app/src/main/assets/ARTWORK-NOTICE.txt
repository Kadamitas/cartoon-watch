Cartoon Watch artwork metadata

The catalog contains small image URLs and source credits, not bundled cover files.
Images remain subject to the rights of their respective owners. Provider names
identify artwork sources and do not imply endorsement of Cartoon Watch.

anime-offline-database
Contains information from anime-offline-database, maintained by cedya77 as a
continuation of manami-project's anime-offline-database.
https://github.com/cedya77/anime-offline-database
https://github.com/manami-project/anime-offline-database

The AODB-derived artwork URL/title mappings, identifiable by their imageAttribution
field, are available under the Open Database License 1.0. Individual database
contents are available under the Database Contents License 1.0. The extracted
mapping is adapted to WCO title links and normalized titles. Upstream notices and
the applicable license URIs are retained here and in the catalog's source metadata.
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1.0/

TVmaze
TVmaze-derived artwork URL/title mappings, identifiable by imageSource TVmaze,
are adapted from its public animation index and available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Each matching catalog item retains a TVmaze source-page link and attribution.
https://www.tvmaze.com/api
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

These source-specific artwork mappings remain separately identifiable. These data
licenses do not change the licenses of application code, independently sourced
catalog fields, or private episode screenshots stored only on the user's device.
